📜 ESS Survival Rules

Please read and understand the rules before playing.

1 Treat Other Players With Respect

  • Harassment, bullying, or targeted abuse is not permitted.
  • If another player is bothering you, report the situation instead of escalating it.
  • Respect players who have blocked or ignored you.
  • Player reports should be submitted through the server's official support system.
  • Abuse of staff authority should also be reported to server administration.

2 No Griefing

  • Do not intentionally destroy or damage another player's builds.
  • Revenge griefing is not permitted.
  • Lava casting and large-scale unnecessary destruction are prohibited.
  • Resource worlds may have different rules where specifically stated.

3 Keep Communication Appropriate

  • Harassment, hate speech, racism, excessive profanity, and inappropriate content are prohibited.
  • Inappropriate usernames, builds, signs, skins, and renamed items are not allowed.
  • Keep sensitive discussions out of public server chat.
  • Do not use communication features to deliberately bypass moderation.

4 No Hacking, X-Ray, or Exploiting

  • Cheats such as X-Ray, Kill Aura, Fly, automated combat, and similar advantages are prohibited.
  • Performance and cosmetic modifications may be allowed.
  • Information already available through normal Minecraft interfaces may be displayed by permitted minimap or waypoint modifications.
  • Building schematic tools may be allowed, but automated placement is prohibited unless specifically approved.
  • Any newly discovered server or Minecraft exploit must be reported rather than abused.

5 No Automation Tools

  • Botting, automated clicking, key-weight methods, and similar automation are prohibited.
  • Normal farms that require legitimate player interaction are allowed.
  • Fully automated AFK machines may be restricted where they create unfair advantages or excessive server load.

6 No Random PvP

  • PvP must be agreed to by all players involved.
  • Players entering clearly marked PvP areas are considered to have accepted the area's PvP rules.
  • Do not teleport, lure, or trick players into PvP areas against their wishes.
  • If a player attacks you first, the situation may be treated as consent to fight back, but reporting the incident is recommended.

7 English in Public Chat

  • English should be used in public server chat so staff can effectively moderate conversations.
  • Other languages may be used in private messages and approved private/team channels.

8 No Spamming, Begging, or Advertising

  • Do not repeatedly send identical or nearly identical messages.
  • Avoid excessive capitalization and meaningless character spam.
  • Unauthorized external advertising is prohibited.
  • Do not advertise competing Minecraft servers without permission.

9 Claims

  • Claims may expire after extended periods of inactivity.
  • Do not build immediately next to another player's claim without permission.
  • Respect claim boundaries and the wishes of claim owners.
  • Claim disputes should be reported through the official support system.

10 No Scamming, Fraud, or Impersonation

  • Do not intentionally misrepresent items or trades.
  • Players are responsible for understanding the value of items before trading.
  • Do not impersonate another player or staff member.
  • Keep evidence of important trades if a dispute may arise.
  • Real-money trading must only occur through officially approved ESS services.

11 Shop and Economy

  • Do not manipulate shop ratings using alternate accounts.
  • Do not advertise around another player's shop without permission.
  • Fake or misleading items may not be sold.
  • Do not use the economy to exploit unintended server mechanics.
  • Player warps, auction listings, and shops must be used appropriately.

12 Alternate Accounts

  • Alternate accounts may be permitted by ESS.
  • Limits may apply to how many accounts can be online simultaneously.
  • Do not use alternate accounts to gain unfair economic advantages.
  • Never use accounts that do not belong to you.

13 Do Not Evade Punishments

  • Do not use alternate accounts to bypass a ban or other punishment.
  • Do not deliberately circumvent chat restrictions.
  • Attempts to evade moderation may result in additional punishment.

14 Map Art

  • Map art may be created using permitted building tools.
  • Automated placement remains prohibited unless explicitly approved.
  • Do not resell another player's map art without permission.
  • Map-art builds should be properly claimed.

15 Lag Prevention

  • Machines and farms should be designed efficiently.
  • Do not intentionally create unnecessary server lag.
  • Chunk-loading systems may be prohibited.
  • Machines that continuously create and remove large numbers of blocks or fluids may be restricted.
